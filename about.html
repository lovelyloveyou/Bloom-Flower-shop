About Us

The story behind every bloom

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Port of Spain, Bloom Haven started as a small passion project by Alicia Ramsaran, a Trinidad-born floral artist with a dream to bring the beauty of Caribbean blooms to every home and celebration.

Our Story

What began as a modest workshop has blossomed into one of Trinidad & Tobago's most trusted florists, serving thousands of happy customers with fresh, handcrafted arrangements for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and everyday moments of joy.

Today, our team of skilled florists creates over 500 unique arrangements each month, combining local tropical flowers with premium imported varieties to craft designs that are truly one-of-a-kind.
